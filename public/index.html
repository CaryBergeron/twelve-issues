Before the next cold snap, check these three spots

Every January we get the same call: a pipe split overnight, and water is coming through the ceiling. Almost every time, it is one of three places.

The first is the hose bib on the north side of the house. The second is any pipe running along an outside wall in the garage. The third is the line under the kitchen sink when the cabinet doors stay closed.

Open those cabinet doors on cold nights, disconnect garden hoses, and know where your main shutoff is.

Questions

What can I upload?

PDF, Word (DOCX), EPUB, or plain text, up to several files at once. Authors usually upload a manuscript. Businesses often upload a services page, a brochure, past blog posts, or FAQs. No document? Answer the questionnaire instead.

Is my manuscript safe?

Your files are read in your own browser and only the text is sent to us. We use it to write your issues and nothing else. It is never used to train AI models, and we delete it on request.

Which email service does it work with?

All of them. Copy an issue into Mailchimp, Kit, Constant Contact, Substack, or plain Gmail, or download every issue as Markdown or email-ready HTML.

Can I edit the issues?

Yes. They are plain text you own. Change anything, add photos, and send them on your own schedule.

Do I have to send them monthly?

Monthly is the default, and issues are tied to the season. You can choose weekly or every two weeks instead when you set up your project.
